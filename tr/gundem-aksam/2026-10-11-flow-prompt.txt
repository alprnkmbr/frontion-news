PROMPT (İngilizce — Flow/Veo için)
====================================================================

STYLE: Bold black-and-white Turkish editorial cartoon / newspaper engraving
(Leman / Penguen / Uykusuz daily-press line), thick hand-inked contours,
heavy cross-hatching, coarse halftone printing grain, aged newsprint texture.
Monochrome ink palette — deep blacks, warm paper white — with ONE single
deep crimson-red accent (#C41E3A). Vertical 16:9 is WRONG — this must be
HORIZONTAL 16:9 widescreen landscape.

SCENE: A generic, evergreen press/broadcast atmosphere — not tied to any
single news day. A dark editorial newsroom-ish background: an inked city
skyline of anonymous towers and domes dissolving into a faint hand-drawn
world-map grid and translucent wireframe data-lines drifting slowly across
the sky (global news / financial flows). Scattered loose newspaper pages and
fine ink particles float gently. On the edges/corners, generic press props in
the same ink style: an inkwell and quill, a fountain pen, stacked folded
newspapers, a vintage typewriter, a radio/microphone — arranged as framing
decoration, leaving the CENTRE-LEFT area relatively calm and darker for overlay
text. Optionally, in the far corners only, faceless anonymous silhouette
figures (a journalist in a hat, a hand holding a pen) rendered as pure black
ink silhouettes — NO faces, NO identifiable individuals, NO ethnic/national
stereotypes, no orientalist costuming whatsoever.

TEXT — must appear ON-SCREEN, permanently visible for the whole clip, in a
fixed corner banner that does NOT move:
  - Primary: the exact words  FRONTION TÜRKÇE
  - Below it: the exact words  GÜNÜN ÖZETİ
  - Correct Turkish characters. Elegant classic serif typeface (Playfair
    Display / newspaper masthead feel). Ink-black lettering, with ONE thin
    deep red (#C41E3A) underline rule under the title.
  - The banner stays static and identical at the start and end of the loop.
  - NO other readable text anywhere in the frame.

MOTION: very slow, continuous, seamless loop — gentle parallax drift of the
background layers, slow float of paper pages, soft drifting ink particles,
a single slowly breathing red radar/pulse dot. Calm but tense, satirical,
editorial, serious. No camera cuts, no snaps, no fast motion.

NO: photorealistic faces, identifiable people, orientalist/Ottoman-style
figures, realistic military hardware, detailed flags, gore, colour other than
the single crimson accent, foreign/incorrect lettering, logos, watermarks,
fast cuts, hard zooms, vertical portrait framing.
