PROMPT (İngilizce — Flow/Veo için)
====================================================================

STYLE: Bold black-and-white Turkish editorial cartoon / newspaper engraving
(Leman / Penguen / Uykusuz daily-press line), thick hand-inked contours,
heavy cross-hatching, coarse halftone printing grain. BRIGHT, AIRY, LIGHT
palette — the scene sits on WARM NEWSPRINT PAPER (off-white / cream), NOT a
dark or noir background. Monochrome ink drawing on light paper, with ONE
single deep crimson-red accent (#C41E3A). HORIZONTAL 16:9 widescreen landscape
(vertical portrait framing is WRONG).

SCENE: A bright, cheerful, daytime editorial newsroom / newspaper desk drawn
as a lively ink cartoon on cream newsprint — NOT dark, NOT moody, NOT noir.
An open newspaper spread fills much of the frame: bold masthead-style ink
frames, hand-drawn columns, a hand-inked city skyline of anonymous towers and
domes along the top dissolving into a light, faint hand-drawn world-map grid
and thin sketch data-lines drifting gently across the sky (global news /
financial flows). Scattered loose newspaper pages and fine ink particles float
softly. Framing decoration in the same bright ink style along the edges: a
coffee cup with steam, a fountain pen, an inkwell, stacked folded newspapers,
a vintage typewriter, a small radio/microphone, a pinned "gündem" bulletin
board. Keep the CENTRE-LEFT area calm and open for a text banner. DO NOT
include any detective, hat-wearing, trench-coat, mysterious or noir-styled
figures. If any people appear at all, they must be everyday, neutral,
anonymous and friendly — e.g. a small crowd of ordinary readers with plain
silhouettes in a newspaper cartoon — NEVER mysterious, NEVER secretive, no
faces, no identifiable individuals, no ethnic/national stereotypes, no
orientalist costuming.

TEXT — must appear ON-SCREEN, permanently visible for the whole clip, in a
fixed corner banner that does NOT move:
  - Primary: the exact words  FRONTION TÜRKÇE
  - Below it: the exact words  GÜNÜN ÖZETİ
  - Correct Turkish characters. Elegant classic serif typeface (Playfair
    Display / newspaper masthead feel). Ink-black lettering on the light
    banner, with ONE thin deep red (#C41E3A) underline rule under the title.
  - The banner stays static and identical at the start and end of the loop.
  - NO other readable text anywhere in the frame.

MOTION: very slow, continuous, seamless loop — gentle parallax drift of the
paper layers, slow soft float of newspaper pages, drifting ink particles, a
single slowly breathing red radar/pulse dot. Bright, lively, informative,
lightly satirical, editorial, welcoming. No camera cuts, no snaps, no fast
motion.

LIGHTING: bright, warm, daylight, cheerful. High-key, lots of paper white.
Absolutely NOT dark, NOT shadowy, NOT noir, NOT night-time.

NO: dark/moody/noir atmosphere, detectives, fedoras, trench coats, mysterious
silhouettes, night scenes, photorealistic faces, identifiable people,
orientalist/Ottoman-style figures, realistic military hardware, detailed flags,
gore, colour other than the single crimson accent, foreign/incorrect lettering,
logos, watermarks, fast cuts, hard zooms, vertical portrait framing.
